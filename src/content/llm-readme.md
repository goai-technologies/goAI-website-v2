We typically work as a hands-on product and engineering partner: understanding the problem, designing the AI architecture, and shipping production-ready systems.

## 5. FAQs

**Q1. What does goAI Solutions do?**
goAI Solutions helps companies design, build, and deploy AI-powered products and automation. This includes custom applications, agentic workflows, and integrations that move real metrics – not just proof-of-concepts.

**Q2. Is goAI an AI platform, a product, or a consulting firm?**
goAI combines product thinking + custom engineering. We are not an LLM provider; instead we assemble best-in-class models, tools, and infrastructure into usable products and workflows for each client.

**Q3. Who is a good fit to work with goAI?**
Ideal customers are founders and teams who already see a clear business problem or opportunity where AI can help, want to ship something in weeks (not years), and value a pragmatic engineering partner over a purely advisory role.

**Q4. Where is goAI Solutions based?**
goAI Solutions (GoAI Solutions Private Limited) is based in Bangalore, Karnataka, India, and works remotely with clients.

**Q5. Does goAI build end-user chatbots?**
goAI can build chat- or agent-based interfaces when they are the right UX, but we are not a generic consumer chatbot company. The focus is business workflows and products rather than public, open-ended chat apps.

**Q6. Is goAI a general AI / LLM provider?**
No. goAI does not train or sell its own foundation LLMs. We build on top of existing AI/LLM providers and open-source models, choosing the right stack for each use case, with attention to cost, latency, and data security.
